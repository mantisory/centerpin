ISLANDER PRECISION C5 CENTERPIN REEL

"Out with the Sea Run and In with the long-awaited release of its replacement the C5". Islander's new-relese 5 inch diameter float reel is described as smooth drifts, pinpoint precision, and hook-ups you’ll never forget. Built to redefine your expectations, the Islander C5 takes performance to the next level.

Rotation

The C5 features a precision-machined spindle and a finely tuned bearing system that ensures silky smooth operation, crucial for maintaining control over delicate presentations and subtle drifts. This seamless function is especially valuable when targeting wary fish in clear, fast-moving streams, where even the smallest detail of your setup can make or break the experience. The C5’s bearing system is designed to maintain its performance under various conditions, providing consistent control when it matters most.

5” Diameter

With a 5-inch diameter and large arbor, the C5 speeds up line retrieval, making it effortless to manage long drifts and quickly reel in slack line. This design enhances line management efficiency and helps maintain tension, which is vital for setting hooks and battling fish that make sudden, powerful runs. The large arbor also reduces floating resistance, allowing the reel to smoothly spool out line even in slower-moving water. This combination of optimal diameter and arbor design ensures faster retrieves and improved line handling, making it ideal for anglers who value precision and speed.

Travel Lock

The C5 features a robust travel lock knob that keeps your gear secure during transit—an essential when moving through dense brush or between fishing spots. The lock mechanism is designed with a subtle click, providing tactile feedback when turning it on and off. This feature prevents line spool-out. To prevent premature wear, ensure that you disengage the lock before fishing.

Free Spool Lever

The C5’s intuitive free-spool lever is crafted from solid brass, offering durability and a touch of classic style. Engineered for smooth, effortless transitions between engaged and free-spool settings, the brass lever adds a premium feel. A distinctive flick mechanism allows you to adjust on the fly with just a touch of your finger, keeping your attention on the water instead of your gear. Its low-profile design minimizes accidental engagement, providing anglers with a seamless and distraction-free experience.

Interchangeable Retrieve & Ambidextrous Lever

The C5’s non-directional, interchangeable retrieve system is designed for both right- and left-handed anglers, ready to fish out of the box without the need for tools or disassembly. The ambidextrous lever is ergonomically designed for comfort and balance whether you are right or left handed.

Construction

Crafted from a single block of aerospace-grade aluminum, the C5’s body is precision-shaped on a 5-axis CNC machine and thoughtfully ported for optimal weight and balance. The modern design removes weak points, delivering unmatched strength and a stable feel that experienced anglers appreciate during extensive use. The reel is anodized to resist corrosion and wear, ensuring durability in all conditions. Each reel is meticulously manufactured, tuned and hand-tested by expert builders on Vancouver Island. Giving durability and performance anglers can rely on.

Two-Year Warranty

The C5 comes with a two-year warranty against defects in materials or workmanship (see warranty section for full details). Our commitment to quality is backed by dedicated customer support and easy access to service, so you can fish with confidence knowing that your reel is built to last.

ISLANDER MR3 SALMON MOOCHING REEL

The Islander MR3 is Islander's premier salmon mooching reel- best in the field. It is the results of years of research and development based upon the input of dealers, guides, and pro-staff. Single action reels have long been the choice of most experienced guides and premier lodges on the west cost of British Columbia. Throughout its history, Islander has gained a reputation for longevity and reliability across the fishing community.

Continuing in the Islander tradition of the single action reel, the MR3 utilizes Islander's impressive silky smooth drag system. Drag is supplied by a large 3-inch clutch, which uses a double pawl system for positive clutch lock up. The drag is adjusted from the spool side, hence the nickname "Knuckle-buster" which happens if the angler is not quick enough moving their hand when the fish makes a run for freedom.

The large sculptured drag knob makes for comfortable use under all conditions. A wide range of drag adjustment allows the angler to choose just the right amount of pressure to subdue even the most ambitious finned adversary. Incorporated into the reel are three large, fully sealed stainless steel ball bearings. The MR3 incorporates a large arbor spool for quicker line pickup; this also eliminates the need for the use of backing as filler. Oversized handles are very user friendly and there also flat, paddle style handles available as an option. Every MR3 reel comes with a 2 year warranty against manufacturing defects.
