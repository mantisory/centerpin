Precision and Power for Overhead and Top-Hand-Dominant Casting.

The Affinity DQ series is designed for casters who favor a precise, controlled stroke with a dominant top hand. Unlike traditional Underhand (UH) and Scandi tapers, which load deeply from the bottom of the rod, the DQ tapers feature a stiff lower section and a progressively softer tip. This design enhances overhead casting efficiency and allows for a more direct energy transfer when using a top-hand-dominant technique.
