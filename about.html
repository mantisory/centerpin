ABOUT US

Please Conserve Our Magnificent Fishery. Practice C.R.R (Catch.Revive.Release)

Centerpin Angling® (centerpinangling.com) / (centrepinangling.com) is a Verified Merchant-Specialist of quality Floatfishing Tackle. Outfitting starters & professional steelheaders worldwide. Our company's structure is built through years of experience in sport float fishing using a centerpin reel, spanning from the mid 80's to date with dedicated dawn to dusk fishing in rivers of the Great Lakes Regions of Ontario, Canada & North Eastern United States. Float Fishing is All We Do! Ultimately this gives us the edge in product innovation, selection, expertise to help us offer our customers' quality products, know-how, a long standing commitment to best prices and customer service.
