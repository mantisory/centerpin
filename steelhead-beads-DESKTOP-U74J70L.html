Centerpin Angling® STEELHEAD BEADS™

CAC steelhead beads are exclusive and unique. Most are painted and prepped in-shop in unique fish catching colors. We also Offer bead kits in the "Ontario Selects" or "New York Selects" assortment, complete in a 18 compartment box containing 270 beads in 9 colors in 8 & 10mm size,complete with an Identity label for easy referencing and refilling your bead-box. Our beads are finished in various Iridescent or matte finish as shown. We also offer the refill packs to replenish your stock or just add to your personal selection. C.A.C. steelhead beads may be rigged using a trout bead knot or pegged with a toothpick. We also offer the silicone pegs(listed above) which make rigging a breeze. When rigging and drifting beads .. the bead is usually held at a distance of about 1-2 inches above the hook to avoid foul hooking or injuring steelhead. Beads offer an excellent stray single egg presentations and also complement in tandem rigging(where allowed- Check your regs) to other lure and bait enticing-rigging. These may also be used for rigs and heads on steelhead jigs, using our strong jig pins, quality jig hooks, marabou or rabbit fur creating steelhead jigs and or lures. M. I. in USA & Canada.
