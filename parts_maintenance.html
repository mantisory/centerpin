CENTREPIN REEL REPLACEMENT BEARINGS

Our in-stock bearings are OEM (original equipment manufacturer) where available or higher quality, and in a bearing class of ABEC 5 or higher depending on application. We carefully select all bearings to insure smooth running, low-torque start-up and smooth spin of your centerpin. In "our opinion" and in cases noticed over the years, it's the outer bearing which usually obtains the most wear and load while fishing and batting of the reel's drum, as opposed to the inner back-plate side bearing. Lack of performance-spin of your center pin reel may be due to this bearing needing replacing and showing most wear due to rust-pitted balls and ring surfaces resulting from the harsh elements and the reel sitting for longer periods of time not being used.

In addition, we can supply any bearing for any reel made! Either in oem or a custom reel bearing in a class rating of ABEC 3-9. Please email us stating application/size, rating and preferred brand for a quote. We will respond in a timely manner. We also list a great selection of stocked bearings bellow for some of the most used centerpin reels.

INSTALATION INSTRUCTIONS:

1. Remove original reel foot, by 1st removing the three small perimiter screws. Next back off the two centerscrewws holding down the clicker spring as needed, until the foot releases away from the plate(if needed) You do not need to completely remove these two clicker spring screws)

2. Once removed, wipe down the area, line up the new aluminum and thread-start the small flat head slot perimeter screws and when all are started begin tightening them dow evenly un till snug(do not over tighten) Next tighten down the two clicker screws lined up with the foot bores, paying close attention that there isn't any extreme resistance getting them snug into the bore. During this stage you will also need to check the spring, and hold it straight so that it looks uniform on each side when finished tightening down. NOTE: Some minor modifications may be required on 4.5 inch models (The inner clicker screws may not line up with the clearance holes on the plate, therefore these two screws may need to be shortened (filled down) and only thread into the plate and not the foot, which to then clear the aluminum foot, can be filled down to be flush with the plate) allowing the new foot to fit flush with the plate. For any questions or concerns prior to purchasing call and ask for Jim at the shop.

3. Finish off the installation, by using a small thin straight edge to lift off the decal at the back of the original graphite foot (go slow) and once removed, coat the underside with some clear silicone adhesive and position on the new aluminum foot -align it up- and begin to apply pressure from center out to stick evenly on the new foot and wipe excess down gently and allow to dry once satisfied.
