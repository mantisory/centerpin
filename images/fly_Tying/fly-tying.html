Our pins are solid brass or solid brass-nickel plate finishes, for rust proof resistant and deter rust or staining of the fur on the jig or any corrosion caused from water to weaken the hook and break on a fish. Our pins are custom matched for best fit and work with any size bead we offer. A rounded pin head offers uniform fit on the bead and not breach through the bead hole. The pin's unique size increments complement the size of hook used, allowing minimal interference not to alter the natural flex and strength of the hook once tied on the hook shank. CAC Steelhead jig pins offer unmatched tensile strength to hold the bead secure and straight on the hook. They are practically unbendable with your hands, which is crucial for best presentation and longevity of the jig. Available in nickel(only #1) or brass finish. Note: For best fit use #1 pin for securing beads on hooks ranging in size #8, 6, 4, 2 & 1. Use #2 or #3 pin for hook #1/0, 2/0 & 3/0. (#2 & #3 pin will also fit bead bores ranging in size 5/32" to 3/8" ) For hook sizes above #3/0 use #3 or #4 pin. All sizes are packaged 25 pins per poly bag. M. I. Germany.
