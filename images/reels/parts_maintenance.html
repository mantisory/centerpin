CENTREPIN REEL REPLACEMENT BEARINGS

Our in-stock bearings are OEM (original equipment manufacturer) where available or higher quality, and in a bearing class of ABEC 3 or higher depending on application. We carefully select all bearings to insure smooth running, low-torque start-up and smooth spin of your centerpin. In "our opinion" and in cases noticed over the years, it's the outer bearing which usually obtains the most wear and load while fishing and batting of the reel's drum, as opposed to the inner back-plate side bearing. Lack of performance-spin of your center pin reel may be due to this bearing needing replacing and showing most wear due to rust-pitted balls and ring surfaces resulting from the harsh elements and the reel sitting for longer periods of time not being used. In addition, we can supply any bearing for any reel made! Either in oem or a specially specific bearing in a class rating of ABEC 1-9. Please email us stating application/size, rating and preferred brand for a quote. We will respond in a timely manner. We also list a great selection of stocked bearings bellow for some of the most used centerpin reels.

A quick performance-test for any bearing wear may be achieved without the bearings being removal from the spool housing., by simply separating the spool from the back-plate(if able), and once the bearing(s) inner rings are visible in each side of the spool, begin placing the sharpened end of a pencil in the inner ring and while holding the spool ..slowly rotating the pencil back and forth to get the rings moving on the steel balls and simultaneously paying close attention to any excessive noise, rumbling, and any slightest resistance being felt while the races are in motion, deterring best optimal spin indicating bearing replacement.

In addition, we can supply any bearing for any reel made! Either in oem or a custom reel bearing in a class rating of ABEC 3-9. Please email us stating application/size, rating and preferred brand for a quote. We will respond in a timely manner. We also list a great selection of stocked bearings bellow for some of the most used centerpin reels.
